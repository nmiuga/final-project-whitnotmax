# Overall Learnings
I immediately knew I wanted to build something geography-related when given this assignment, so I had to do a lot of research on the Location Services and MapKit functionality of iOS. Integrating widgets was also a bit of a challenge, since I did not know how to set up widgets at all before going into this project. Furthermore, most of the apps and websites I have built were janky prototypes, so working through this app taught me just how much thought goes into creating a publishable-quality app in terms of UI and UX details. The initial functionality that I personally wanted to get out of the app was quickly finished, but thinking about how to balance adding features would make the app compelling for a wider variety of use cases while not compromising on the simplicity of the app was an interesting challenge.

# Challenges
My experience during this app was that AI was great at prototyping an idea I had, but would need manual editing to make it feel how I wanted to. I felt like fixing tiny UI bugs took as much of my time as implementing entire features, since I could not figure out a way to describe the UI bug I was seeing to allow the AI to fix it for me, even taking screenshots and showing what I was seeing. Additionally, I did not realize when starting to write this app that iOS GPS sensors are only accurate to about 15 meters, so I had to account for that in the guiding functionality of the app. When I was testing in the real world, I would set a spot, and then try to return to it, and the app would lead me perfectly to the spot until I got close, and then the directions would often be completely wrong. That issue stumped me for hours until I realized it was a hardware limitation.

# Next steps
There is definitely room to improve and make this app even more polished. If I wanted to do it, I think I would need to sit down and thoroughly understand the different pieces of the app, like the different views and data structures, and make edits by hand, since they are small details that the AI wouldn't be able to help much with (in my experience). It will be interesting to monitor AI development tools in the future to see if they advance enoguh to help with unique bugs and UI problems. I know for sure I will be personally using this app in my own life, and I would like to look into the actual app publishing process/Test Flight program since I feel like this is an app that others would genuinely find useful.
